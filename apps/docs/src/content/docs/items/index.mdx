An **item** in Hisaabo is a product or service that you sell or purchase. Items appear in your invoice line items, and stock quantities are automatically adjusted when you create invoices.

## Item types

| Type | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Physical goods with trackable stock (fabric, electronics, stationery, etc.) |
| **Service** | Work or labour with no physical stock (installation, consulting, delivery charges) |

Service items have stock tracking disabled — their stock quantity is always shown as N/A.

## Item modes

Hisaabo supports three item modes. Choose the right mode when creating the item:

| Mode | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | Single unit, single price. Most items fit here. |
| **Alternate Units** | Same item sold or purchased in different units (e.g. cloth sold by metre but tracked in rolls) |
| **Variants** | Same item with multiple configurations (e.g. t-shirt in S/M/L x Red/Blue) |

See [Variants](/items/variants) and [Units & Conversions](/items/units-and-conversions) for the advanced modes.

:::note[Mobile limitation]
The mobile app only supports creating **Simple** mode items. To create items with variants or alternate units, use the web app. Once created, variant and alternate-unit items can be viewed and used on invoices from the mobile app.
:::

## Adding an item

<ForDesktop>
Go to **Items** in the sidebar and click **+ Add Item** (or press <kbd>N</kbd>). A slide-over panel opens with collapsible Disclosure sections for each group of fields: Item Type, Basic Info, Pricing, Unit, and Stock.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Tap **Items** in the bottom tab bar (if visible) or navigate to the Items section, then tap the floating **+** button. A full-screen form opens with stacked cards for each section:

- **Item Type** — Product or Service toggle
- **Basic Info** — Name, SKU, category, description
- **Pricing** — Sale price, purchase price, and tax preset chips (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) for quick GST rate selection
- **Unit** — Tap to open a unit picker modal with the full list of supported units
- **Stock** — Opening stock quantity and low-stock alert threshold
</ForMobile>

### Required fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Must be unique within your business (case-insensitive) |
| **Item Type** | Product or Service |
| **Unit** | Base unit of measurement. See [Supported Units](/reference/supported-units). |

### Important fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Sale Price** | Your default selling price per unit (excluding tax if tax-exclusive) |
| **Purchase Price** | Your default buying price per unit |
| **Tax %** | GST rate: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%. Applied to every invoice line item using this item. |
| **Tax Inclusive** | If enabled, the **sale** price already includes GST (the purchase price never does). On a bill the line starts at the tax-exclusive rate, so ₹118.00 including 18% GST bills as ₹100.00 + ₹18.00 = ₹118.00. See [Tax-inclusive pricing](/invoicing/gst-on-invoices#tax-inclusive-pricing). |
| **HSN Code** | Harmonised System of Nomenclature code (for products) or SAC code (for services). Printed on invoices and used in GSTR-1 HSN summary. |

### Stock tracking fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Stock Quantity** | Current stock (opening stock when you first create the item). Updated automatically on invoices. |
| **Low Stock Alert** | When stock falls at or below this number, the item is flagged in the low-stock view. |

### Other fields

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **SKU** | Your internal stock-keeping unit number or barcode. Not required but useful for search. |
| **Category** | Free-text category for grouping items (e.g. "Electronics", "Clothing", "Services"). |
| **Description** | Internal notes about the item. Not shown on invoices. |

## Filtering and searching items

<ForDesktop>
The items list provides a search bar at the top and a **SegmentedControl** to filter by type: **All**, **Products**, or **Services**. Items with stock at or below the low-stock threshold display a low-stock badge next to the stock quantity. You can click the **Export** button to download the item list as a CSV file.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
The items list provides a search bar at the top and horizontal chip filters for **All**, **Products**, **Services**, and **Low Stock**. Tap the **Low Stock** chip to see only items that need reordering. CSV export is not available on mobile.
</ForMobile>

## How stock is tracked

Stock is stored as `NUMERIC(15,3)` in the database — it supports fractional quantities (for example, 2.5 kg). The system updates stock atomically inside the same transaction as each invoice:

- **Sale invoice created** → stock quantity decreases
- **Purchase invoice created** → stock quantity increases
- **Invoice line items edited** → original stock change is reversed, new change is applied
- **Invoice cancelled/deleted** → stock change is **not** automatically reversed (see note below)

:::caution[Deletion does not reverse stock]
When you cancel or delete an invoice, the stock adjustment is not automatically reversed. If you need to add stock back, use the **Stock Adjustment** feature (Items → select item → Adjust Stock) or create a purchase invoice for the returned quantity.
:::

## Stock adjustments

You can manually adjust stock outside of invoices. Common uses:
- Opening stock entry when migrating to Hisaabo
- Physical inventory count correction
- Writing off damaged or expired goods

<ForDesktop>
To adjust stock: open the item detail in the slide-over panel, then click **Adjust Stock** in the footer. A modal appears where you enter the adjustment details.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
To adjust stock: open the item detail screen, then tap **Adjust Stock**. A modal appears where you enter the adjustment details.
</ForMobile>

Enter the following fields:
- **Quantity change**: positive to add stock, negative to remove stock
- **Reason**: for example, "Physical count correction" or "Goods damaged"
- **Adjustment date**: defaults to today, but you can backdate the adjustment

Every adjustment is logged with the previous stock, new stock, quantity change, reason, date, and the user who made the change. You can view the full adjustment history on the item detail page. For items with variants, you can adjust stock per variant individually.

## Low-stock alerts

Items where `stockQuantity <= lowStockAlert` appear in the **Low Stock** filter on the items list. Use this to quickly see what needs reordering.

:::tip
Set a low-stock alert threshold on every item you actively purchase. Check the low-stock view weekly before placing purchase orders.
:::

## Update prices from a bill

It is painful to open each item to change its price. When you make or edit an **invoice** and change the **rate** of a line that is linked to a catalog item, Hisaabo asks once, when you save, whether to update the item's saved price too.

<ForDesktop>
Change the **Price** of a line, then click **Create Invoice** (or **Save Changes**). A box "Update saved prices?" lists every changed item as old price to new price, for example:

- Basmati Rice (5 kg) ₹450.00 → ₹480.00
- Ghee 1L ₹620.00 → ₹600.00

Every item has a checkbox, checked by default. **Update item prices** saves the bill and then updates the checked items. **Just this bill** saves the bill and leaves the catalog alone. **Esc** (or the ×) goes back to the form without saving anything.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Change the **Rate** of a line, then tap **Create Invoice** (or **Save**). A sheet "Update saved prices?" lists every changed item as old price to new price, with a checkbox per item (all checked). **Update item prices** saves the invoice and then the checked prices; **Just this bill** saves the invoice only. The close button or the back gesture returns to the form without saving.
</ForMobile>

How it decides:

- **Only invoices ask.** A sale invoice proposes the selling price, a purchase invoice the purchase price. Quotations, proformas and delivery challans are offers and movement notes, usually priced for one customer (the invoice they become asks); credit notes, debit notes and returns copy the original invoice's rates, so a change there is an adjustment, not a new price.
- **Only a rate you changed.** Opening an old bill and saving it does not offer to revert prices that changed since. A **discount** is never part of the price: a discounted line whose rate you did not touch proposes nothing, and one whose rate you did change proposes that rate, not the discounted amount.
- **The right price.** A line in an alternate unit updates that unit's own price (the 5 kg pack's price, not the per-kg price); a variant line updates that variant's price. If the unit was renamed or removed since, the line is listed as "not offered" instead of guessing.
- **Tax-inclusive items.** The rate on a bill is always tax-exclusive. For an item whose sale price includes GST, the rate you typed (₹105.00 at 18%) is converted back to the inclusive price (₹123.90) before it is compared and saved, and the box says "incl. GST".
- **Who is asked.** Only people who can edit items: the owner, admins and seller managers. Sellers and accountants see no box and nothing changes for them.
- **The bill always saves.** If the price update fails, the bill is already saved and you get a message; nothing is rolled back. Updating twice does nothing the second time.
- **Audit.** Each changed price is written to the audit log with the old price, the new price and the bill it came from. The item's price history is built from your bills, so the bill itself is the record of the price you billed.

The CLI and AI tools can do the same: `hisaabo invoice create --update-item-prices` and the MCP `update_item_prices` option update the saved price of every linked item whose rate you stated differs. See [CLI](/ai/cli/) and [MCP server](/ai/mcp-server/).

## Online store visibility

Each item can be enabled for your [Online Store](/online-store). When enabled, you can set:
- A separate **Store Price** (defaults to sale price if not set)
- A **Store Category** (different from the internal category)
- A **Store Description** (customer-facing, different from internal description)
- A **Sort Order** (controls the order items appear in the store)

Stock management for the store follows the same rules as regular invoices. If the store is running low on stock, it appears in the low-stock view.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have the same item name for different businesses?**
Yes. Item names are unique per business, not across your entire Hisaabo account.

**Can I change an item's mode after creation?**
No. The item mode (simple, alternate units, variants) is fixed at creation time. If you need a different mode, create a new item.

**Does Hisaabo support negative stock?**
Yes, stock can go negative if you sell more than you have in stock. There is no hard block on selling below zero. The low-stock alert and the negative value in the stock column are the signals to notice.

**How do I set the HSN code for a service item?**
Services use **SAC** (Services Accounting Code) rather than HSN. Enter the SAC code in the HSN field — both are 4-8 digit codes and are handled identically by Hisaabo.
