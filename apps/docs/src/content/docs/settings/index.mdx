Settings is where you configure your business profile, invoice numbering, team access, and personal account preferences.

<ForDesktop>
Access settings from the sidebar: **Settings → Business Profile**, **Settings → Team**, or **Settings → Account**.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Access settings from the **More** tab at the bottom, then tap **Settings**.
</ForMobile>

## Business Profile

Found in **Settings → Business Profile**.

Update your business name, legal name, contact details, and address. The most critical fields here are:

- **State Code** — the 2-digit GST state code. Used for CGST/SGST vs IGST determination. See [Create Your Business](/getting-started/create-business#step-2-address).
- **GST Registration Type** — Regular, Composition, or Unregistered.
- **GSTIN** — your GST Identification Number, printed on all invoices and GST reports.

Changes to the business profile take effect immediately on all future documents. Existing invoices are not retrospectively updated.

## Invoice Numbering

Configure the prefix and starting number for each document type:

| Document Type | Default Prefix |
|---|---|
| Invoice | INV |
| Payment | PAY |
| Quotation | QTN |
| Credit Note | CN |
| Delivery Challan | DC |
| Proforma | PI |
| Store Order | ORD |

To reset the counter — for example, at the start of a new financial year — update the **Next Number** field. Note that this does not reset already-used numbers; it just tells the system what the next invoice number will be. Ensure the new starting number is higher than any existing number to avoid conflicts.

## Financial Year

**Financial Year Start Month** controls which month the financial year begins. The default is April (month 4). Change this to 1 (January) for a calendar year, or any other month if your business uses a different cycle.

The financial year start affects:
- Dashboard "this financial year" totals
- The default date range for new reports

## Online Store

See [Online Store Overview](/online-store) for full documentation. Key settings:

- **Enable/Disable Store** — toggle the store on or off
- **Store Slug** — the URL path for your storefront
- **Accent Colour** — brand colour for the storefront
- **WhatsApp Number** — appears as a contact button on the storefront
- **Min Order Amount** — minimum cart value to place an order

## Team

Manage team members from **Settings → Team**. See [Team & Roles](/team) for the full guide.

## Account

<ForDesktop>
Personal account settings and security, found in **Settings → Account**.

- **Profile** — your display name and email. Changing email requires re-verification.
- **Sessions** — view all active sessions across devices with browser, OS, IP address, and last activity. Revoke individual sessions or sign out all other devices.
- **API Keys** — create and manage programmatic access tokens for the CLI and MCP server. Available on paid plans only.
- **Activity Log** — audit trail of all actions (invoice creation, payment recording, party updates, etc.) with user attribution and time filtering (This Week, This Month, All Time).
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Personal account settings, found in **More → Settings → Profile**.

- **Profile** — your display name and email. Changing email requires re-verification.
- **App Lock** — biometric unlock and PIN lock (see below).
</ForMobile>

## App Lock (Mobile)

<ForDesktop>
App lock is a mobile-only feature. Switch to the **Mobile** view using the toggle in the header to see the full guide.
</ForDesktop>

<ForMobile>
Hisaabo can lock the app when it's not in use, requiring fingerprint, face recognition, or a 4-digit PIN to unlock. This prevents unauthorized access to your business data if someone else picks up your phone.

### First-time setup prompt

After your first login, Hisaabo shows a **"Secure your app"** bottom sheet on the dashboard. You have three options:

1. **Enable Fingerprint / Face ID** — authenticates with your device's biometric sensor. After enabling, you're optionally asked to also set a backup PIN.
2. **Set up PIN instead** — creates a 4-digit numeric PIN for unlocking. Useful if your device doesn't support biometrics or you prefer a PIN.
3. **Skip for now** — dismisses the prompt. The app remains unlocked. You won't be prompted again automatically.

### Enabling app lock later

If you skipped the initial prompt, you can enable app lock at any time:

1. Go to **More → Settings → Profile**
2. Scroll to the **Security** section
3. Toggle **Fingerprint** (or **Face ID**) on — the system biometric prompt appears to verify your identity
4. Optionally, tap **Set PIN** to add a backup PIN

### How unlocking works

When the app launches (or returns from background), the lock screen appears:

- **Biometric mode** — a fingerprint icon is shown. Tap it to authenticate. If biometric fails 3 times and you have a PIN configured, the app switches to PIN mode automatically.
- **PIN mode** — a numeric keypad appears. Enter your 4-digit PIN to unlock.
- You can switch between biometric and PIN modes using the links on the lock screen.
- A **Sign out** link at the bottom lets you sign out entirely if needed.

### Managing app lock

From **More → Settings → Profile → Security**:

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Enable biometrics | Toggle the **Fingerprint / Face ID** switch on |
| Disable biometrics | Toggle the switch off |
| Set or change PIN | Tap **Set PIN** (or **Change PIN**) and enter a new 4-digit PIN twice |
| Remove PIN | Tap **Remove PIN** and confirm |

:::tip[Privacy, not security]
App lock is a local privacy feature — it prevents casual access to your phone. Your actual account security is handled by your session token stored in the device's secure keychain. App lock does not encrypt your data; it adds a visual barrier to opening the app.
:::
</ForMobile>

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change my business's GSTIN?**
Yes. Update it in Business Profile. All future invoices will show the new GSTIN. Existing invoices (already generated PDFs) are not affected unless you regenerate the PDF.

**I want to start fresh invoice numbering for the new financial year. How?**
Go to Settings → Invoice Numbering and set the Next Invoice Number to 1 (and adjust the prefix if you want to include the year, for example `INV-25-26`). This will produce `INV-25-26-00001` as the next invoice.

**Can I change the currency?**
Hisaabo defaults to INR for all monetary values. The currency field exists in the database but the UI always displays amounts as INR. Multi-currency support is not available in the current version.
