Hisaabo is designed to make GST compliance as automatic as possible. This page gives you an overview of how GST flows through the system from invoice creation to report generation.

## How GST is calculated on invoices

See [GST on Invoices](/invoicing/gst-on-invoices) for the full explanation. In summary:

1. You set a tax rate (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%) on each item.
2. You set your business's 2-digit state code in Settings.
3. You set each party's 2-digit state code on their party record.
4. When you create an invoice, Hisaabo compares state codes to determine CGST+SGST (same state) or IGST (different states).

## GST report generation

Hisaabo generates two key GST reports: **GSTR-1** and **GSTR-3B**. Both are computed from your invoice and payment data — there is no manual data entry into the reports.

Reports cover outward supplies (sales) for GSTR-1 and both outward (sales) and inward (purchases) supplies for the ITC calculation in GSTR-3B.

:::note
GST reports are available for **all** businesses — both GST-registered and unregistered. For unregistered businesses, the reports show total sales and purchase figures without GST breakdown. For registered businesses, full CGST/SGST/IGST splits are shown.
:::

## GSTR-1

**GSTR-1** is the monthly return for outward supplies (sales). Hisaabo generates it with the following sections:

| Section | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **B2B** | Sale invoices to GST-registered parties (parties with a GSTIN) |
| **B2C Large** | Inter-state sale invoices to unregistered parties exceeding ₹2.5 lakh |
| **B2C Small** | Intra-state and small inter-state sales to unregistered parties, aggregated by tax rate |
| **HSN Summary** | Total quantity and value sold, broken down by HSN code |
| **Credit Notes** | Credit notes and sales returns issued during the period |
| **Debit Notes** | Sale-side debit notes issued during the period (debit notes and returns you raise against a supplier are purchase-side and are **not** outward supplies, so they never appear here) |

See [GSTR-1](/gst/gstr1) for detailed instructions.

## GSTR-3B

**GSTR-3B** is the monthly summary return. Hisaabo generates:

| Section | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **3.1 Outward Supplies** | Taxable value and tax by IGST, CGST, SGST, **net of the month's credit notes and sales returns and including its sale-side debit notes** |
| **3.2 Inter-state to unregistered** | State-wise IGST on large inter-state B2C supplies |
| **4 ITC (Input Tax Credit)** | GST on purchase invoices, **less the GST on purchase returns and purchase-side debit notes** of the month |
| **Tax Payable** | Output tax (from sales), net of credit notes and including sale-side debit notes |
| **Net Tax** | Tax payable minus ITC = tax you owe to the government (negative = credit carried forward) |

GSTR-1 lists your invoices at their full value and the notes in their own sections; GSTR-3B nets them, so the two returns differ by the notes of the month, as they should. GST is split into CGST and SGST **line by line**, the same way the e-invoice (IRP) payload does: for each line CGST is half the tax rounded half-up and SGST is the remainder, so an odd paisa on a line goes to CGST (a line with ₹0.29 of GST is CGST ₹0.15 + SGST ₹0.14). The invoice, the books, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9, the PDF and the e-way bill all use this one rule, so CGST + SGST always equals the tax charged and the sections of GSTR-1 tie to its totals.

:::note[Past months changed in v0.10.1]
Before v0.10.1, GSTR-3B ignored credit notes, returns and debit notes on the output side, added purchase returns to ITC, and rounded the odd paisa up for both CGST and SGST (a different split from the invoice and the e-invoice). Reports are recomputed every time you open them, so months you have already filed may now show different figures. Compare them with what was filed; see [Upgrading](https://github.com/hisaabo/hisaabo/blob/main/docs/UPGRADING.md).
:::

## CSV export

GSTR-1 can be exported as a CSV file formatted for upload to the GST portal. Each section (B2B, B2CS, etc.) is exported as a separate section within the CSV.

To export: open the GSTR-1 report for the desired month and click **Export CSV**.

## Current limitations

- Exports are in a generic CSV format. The official GST portal requires JSON upload or the offline utility. You may need to reformat the CSV before uploading.
- Zero-rated supplies (SEZ, export of goods) are not separately tracked — they appear as regular inter-state supplies.
- Composition scheme dealers do not have GSTR-4 generation. Composition businesses can use Hisaabo for record-keeping but must prepare their GSTR-4 manually.
- The HSN summary uses HSN code `0000` for line items where the item does not have an HSN code set.

## Filing workflow (recommended)

<ForBusiness>
At the end of each month:

1. Open **GST** in the left navigation and pick the month.
2. Glance at the GSTR-1 totals — do they match roughly what you sold? If a number looks off, the most common cause is a cancelled invoice or a party with the wrong state code.
3. Export the CSV and send it to your CA. That's it on your side — your CA handles the actual portal upload.
4. Check the **GSTR-3B** tab to see your net tax liability before the 20th, so you know how much to keep in the bank.
</ForBusiness>

<ForCA>
:::tip[CA filing workflow]
1. Open the client's business, select **GST** → **GSTR-1**, pick the month.
2. Verify B2B invoice count and aggregate tax against the client's books. Cancelled invoices are excluded; draft invoices are **included** if dated in the period — flag drafts before filing.
3. Export the CSV. Re-format into the GST Offline Utility's template (or use your firm's conversion macro) and generate the JSON.
4. Upload JSON to the GST portal by the **11th**. File GSTR-3B by the **20th** after reviewing ITC in the 3B tab.
5. For amendments, correct the invoice in Hisaabo, re-generate GSTR-1, and report the delta in the next month's amendment tables on the portal.
:::
</ForCA>

## Frequently asked questions

**My GSTIN is not showing on the invoice PDF. What is wrong?**
Go to Settings → Business Profile and confirm that:
1. Your GST Registration Type is set to "Regular" or "Composition" (not "Unregistered").
2. Your GSTIN is entered correctly in the GSTIN field.

**A sale invoice is not appearing in the GSTR-1 report. Why?**
Cancelled invoices are excluded from all GST reports. Check the invoice status. Also check that the invoice date falls within the reporting period and that the document type is "invoice" (quotations and delivery challans are not included in GSTR-1).

**How do I handle invoices for the previous month that I created this month?**
GSTR-1 uses the invoice date, not the creation date. If you create an invoice today but set the invoice date to last month, it will appear in last month's GSTR-1 report.
