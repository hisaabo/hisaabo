## Quick Start

1. **[Sign up free](https://hisaabo.in)** or **[self-host with Docker](/getting-started/self-hosting/)** — your choice
2. **[Create your business](/getting-started/create-business/)** — set up your company profile, GST details, and bank info
3. **[Create your first invoice](/invoicing/create-invoice/)** — generate a GST-compliant invoice with automatic tax calculation
4. **[Import existing data](/getting-started/import-data/)** — bring in your parties, items, and invoices from MyBillBook or CSV
