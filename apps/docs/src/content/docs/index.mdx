## Why Hisaabo?

Hisaabo (from Hindi: हिसाब — accounts) is a complete invoicing and business management platform purpose-built for Indian businesses. Self-hosted or cloud, it gives you full control over your financial data.

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="GST-Compliant Invoicing" icon="document">
    Create professional sale and purchase invoices with automatic CGST/SGST/IGST calculation based on state codes. Generate GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Party Ledgers" icon="list-format">
    Track outstanding balances, view running ledger statements, and manage receivables and payables across all your customers and suppliers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Multi-Business Support" icon="seti:folder">
    Manage multiple businesses from a single account. Each business has its own invoices, parties, items, and reports — completely isolated.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Online Store" icon="laptop">
    Launch a branded online storefront for your business. Customers can browse your catalog and place orders directly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Self-Hosted" icon="setting">
    Your data stays on your server. Deploy with Docker in minutes. No vendor lock-in, no monthly fees, no data sharing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Multi-Platform" icon="phone">
    Self-hosted gives you a fully-featured, responsive web app accessible from any browser on any device. Hisaabo Cloud adds native Android/iOS apps and a desktop app (macOS/Windows/Linux).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Quick Start

1. **[Self-host with Docker](/getting-started/self-hosting/)** — up and running in 5 minutes
2. **[Create your business](/getting-started/create-business/)** — set up your company profile, GST details, and bank info
3. **[Create your first invoice](/invoicing/create-invoice/)** — generate a GST-compliant invoice with automatic tax calculation
4. **[Import existing data](/getting-started/import-data/)** — bring in your parties, items, and invoices from MyBillBook or CSV
