## Quick Start

<ForBusiness>
**For business owners:**

1. **[Sign up free](https://hisaabo.in)** or **[self-host with Docker](/getting-started/self-hosting/)** — your choice
2. **[Create your business](/getting-started/create-business/)** — set up your company profile, GST details, and bank info
3. **[Create your first invoice](/invoicing/create-invoice/)** — generate a GST-compliant invoice with automatic tax calculation
4. **[Import existing data](/getting-started/import-data/)** — bring in your parties, items, and invoices from MyBillBook or CSV
</ForBusiness>

<ForCA>
**For CA firms:**

1. **[Self-host](/getting-started/self-hosting/)** or use **[Hisaabo Cloud](https://hisaabo.in)** — one instance for all your clients
2. **[Create a business per client](/getting-started/create-business/)** — GST details, state code, and Chart of Accounts auto-seed
3. **[Invite your client](/team/invitations/)** — they create invoices from their phone, you see the data live
4. **[Import bank statements](/banking/)** — auto-match 70%+ of transactions across 10 Indian banks
5. **[Generate GST returns](/gst/)** — GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B auto-generate from invoice data
</ForCA>

<ForDev>
**For developers:**

1. **Clone and run locally** — `git clone`, `docker compose up -d`, `pnpm install && pnpm db:push && pnpm dev`
2. **Explore the API** — 200+ typed tRPC endpoints at `localhost:3000`. Full reference at [api.hisaabo.in](https://api.hisaabo.in)
3. **Connect an AI agent** — install `@hisaabo/mcp` for Claude Desktop, or use the [CLI](/ai/cli/) for terminal automation
4. **Read the architecture** — [Hono + tRPC + Drizzle + PostgreSQL](/getting-started/) with typed contracts end-to-end
</ForDev>
